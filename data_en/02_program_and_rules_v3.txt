SECTION 2: PROGRAMME, SCHEDULE AND PARTICIPATION CONDITIONS

What exactly will we do throughout our days in the mountains?
Throughout your days in the mountains, you can expect morning mindfulness practices, group meetings, art therapy and meditation. There will also be time for gentle walks in nature and rest.

Will we go on long, strenuous hikes?
No, we will not go on long, strenuous hikes. The programme includes only gentle, comfortable and restorative walks in nature.

How does art therapy work at the retreat?
Art therapy at the retreat takes the form of creative practices. You will use materials such as paint and paper to explore your inner experiences and emotions in a completely safe environment.

Do you have group sessions, or is all the work individual?
Not all the work is individual. We offer both supportive group sessions, where we talk and share insights, and individual practices for deeper self-exploration.

Will I have the chance to spend time alone in silence?
Yes, you will have the chance to spend time alone in silence. The schedule is designed with your well-being in mind, with personal time set aside for rest and reflection.

Do I have to take part in every practice if I just want to lie down one day?
No, you do not have to take part in every practice. If you simply want to lie down, you can take a break. We recommend following the programme, but nothing is forced—the main rule of the retreat is to be gentle with yourself.

Is this mainly about rest and relaxation, or will there be difficult emotional work?
It is a balance of both. The retreat combines rest in peaceful natural surroundings with thoughtful, deep inner psychological work. It is not intended to be strenuous or exhausting.

Will I need to keep a journal during the programme?
Yes, you will need to keep a journal during the programme. Journaling is an important part of the retreat. We will provide special workbooks for recording your thoughts and insights.

Will we visit sights in the Dolomites?
No, we will not visit sights in the Dolomites. Our retreat focuses on inner work and practices in nature, rather than traditional sightseeing.

Is there any support after the retreat ends?
Yes, support is available after the retreat ends. You will leave with a set of practical materials and self-help techniques to use at home. You will also have access to a support chat.

How many people will be in my group?
There will be no more than 10 participants in the group, including you. The group size is strictly limited to keep the setting small and personal.

Are accommodation and meals included in the price?
Yes, accommodation and meals are fully included in the full participation format with the in-person retreat.

Which airport should I book my flight to?
You should book your flight to Innsbruck Airport (INN).

How will we get from the airport to the retreat venue?
We will travel from the airport to the retreat venue by an organised transfer. The transfer will meet the group at Innsbruck Airport at 12:00 on the first day of the programme.

What clothes will I need? Should I bring evening dresses?
You do not need to bring evening dresses. You will need comfortable clothing: tracksuit bottoms, leggings, loose T-shirts for practices and comfortable clothes for walks.

What footwear is best to bring?
It is best to bring hiking boots or trainers with good grip for walks, as well as a separate pair of comfortable shoes to wear indoors.

It can be cold in the mountains. Should I bring warm clothes?
Yes, warm clothes are essential. Mountain weather can change quickly, so bring a fleece, a windproof jacket and warm socks for evening practices.

What cosmetics or toiletries should I bring?
Bring your usual personal toiletries, a personal first-aid kit and sunscreen (SPF 30+).

Do I need to bring my own exercise books, notebooks and pens for the practices?
You do not need to bring exercise books or notebooks, as we provide special workbooks. However, you are welcome to bring your favourite pen for taking notes.

What sun protection should I bring?
Bring sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher.

What is the digital detox you recommend?
A digital detox means minimising your use of phones and the internet during the retreat. We recommend this to reduce distractions from information overload and help you pay more attention to yourself.

Will there be internet access, and can I work in the evenings?
There is internet coverage, and access may be available. However, we strongly recommend not working in the evenings. Please let your colleagues know in advance and set this time aside just for yourself, without work.

Can I call my family during the retreat?
Yes, you can call your family during the retreat. However, we recommend setting aside a short time for this so that you can stay focused on your inner work.

Can I use my phone to record videos of other participants during meditation?
No, you cannot use your phone to record videos of other participants during meditation. Photography and video recording during practices are strictly limited to protect privacy and maintain an atmosphere of trust.

Why do you strictly limit the group to 10 people?
We strictly limit the group to 10 people because keeping it small is essential to us. This helps maintain an atmosphere of trust and allows the facilitator to give each participant individual attention.
