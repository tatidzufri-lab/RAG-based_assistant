SECTION 1: PRODUCT OVERVIEW, CONCEPT AND AUDIENCE

Question: What exactly is the Know Yourself retreat?
Answer: The Know Yourself retreat is an intimate 5-day programme for women in the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy. The Know Yourself retreat is designed for those who want to slow down, listen to themselves and regain inner clarity, combining rest with deep psychological work.

Question: Where exactly will the programme take place?
Answer: The retreat programme will take place in the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy.

Question: How many days does the trip last?
Answer: The trip lasts exactly 5 days. The in-person part of the retreat and the entire mountain trip take 5 days.

Question: Is your retreat just an in-person trip, or is there some preparation?
Answer: Our retreat includes both preparation and an in-person trip. The format consists of online preparation with video modules, exercises and a chat, followed by a 5-day in-person trip to the mountains.

Question: What does the online part of the programme include?
Answer: The online part of the programme includes video modules, practical exercises such as journaling and meditation, and communication in a support chat.

Question: Does your programme include esoteric practices, astrology or tarot?
Answer: Our programme does not include esoteric practices, astrology or tarot. We do not use esoteric practices. We rely exclusively on scientific existential analysis, art therapy and mindfulness meditation practices.

Question: What methods do you use in your work with participants?
Answer: Our work with participants is based on existential analysis, art therapy, group sessions and mindfulness practices such as meditation.

Question: What will I gain by the time I return home?
Answer: By the time you return home, you will have a clearer sense of direction in life and inner stability. You will also have answers to the question “Where do I go from here?” and a set of practical tools to help maintain this state.

Question: Do you provide any materials so I can continue the practices at home?
Answer: Yes, we provide materials so you can continue the practices at home. You will receive workbooks, journaling exercises, audio meditations and specific self-help tools to continue practising at home.

Question: Can a man attend the retreat?
Answer: Men cannot attend the retreat. The Know Yourself retreat is a programme exclusively for women, designed to create an intimate space with a strong sense of trust.

Question: Who is this programme mainly for?
Answer: This programme is mainly for women who are tired of constant multitasking, feel they have lost their inner stability, are experiencing burnout or simply want to understand where to go next.

Question: I am a mother, my children are older, and I no longer know who I am. Is this right for me?
Answer: Yes, the retreat is an ideal fit if you are a mother whose children are older and you no longer know who you are. The programme will help you reconnect with yourself beyond your family roles and find new sources of personal meaning.

Question: I am a manager, I am very burned out at work, and I am used to controlling everything. Will I be able to relax?
Answer: Yes, you will be able to relax, even if you are a manager, are burned out at work and are used to controlling everything. The programme is designed to help you safely let go of control and fully rest and recharge.

Question: Is the retreat right for me if I am facing a difficult life decision and do not know which direction to take?
Answer: The retreat is absolutely right for you if you are facing a difficult life decision and do not know which direction to take. It gives you time to pause and tools to listen to your own sense of direction and identify your next step.

Question: I have never done yoga and do not know how to meditate. Will I be able to manage?
Answer: You will certainly be able to manage, even if you have never done yoga and do not know how to meditate. The programme is fully adapted to all participants, regardless of their experience.

Question: Do I need a background in psychology or experience with therapy to take part?
Answer: You do not need a background in psychology or experience with therapy to take part. No special preparation is required.

Question: I am looking for a fun trip to drink wine in the mountains and enjoy myself. Will I find this interesting?
Answer: This will not interest you if you are looking for a fun trip to drink wine in the mountains and enjoy yourself. Our retreat involves deep psychological work and self-exploration. It is not an entertainment-focused trip.

Question: Is the programme right for me if I feel creatively blocked and have no inspiration?
Answer: The programme is a very good fit if you feel creatively blocked and have no inspiration. Art therapy, nature and quiet will help you reconnect with your creative potential.

Question: I am afraid my concerns will seem silly compared with other people's. How safe is your space?
Answer: Our space is completely safe, and your concerns will not seem silly compared with other people's. We take great care to create a safe, non-judgmental space in an intimate group of up to 10 people, where every experience matters.

Question: I am an introvert and find it hard to connect with new people. Will I feel comfortable?
Answer: You will feel very comfortable, even if you are an introvert and find it hard to connect with new people. The format includes plenty of quiet time alone, and the small group allows for gentle, considerate interaction.

Question: What can you do?
Answer: I can provide information about the programme, which includes practical tools to maintain your sense of well-being after the retreat, materials to continue the practices at home, and support to help you relax and recharge after burnout. The programme is adapted to all experience levels, including those who have never tried yoga or meditation.
